Our Story

La Piazza Amsterdam has opened its doors in 1982 and is known for its classic, no-nonsense Northern-Italian food and pleasant atmosphere. Our restaurant is located in the heart of Amsterdam with a beautiful view on the Dam Square.

At La Piazza, our goal is to provide our guests with the most authentic Italian experience outside of Italy in terms of food, service and atmosphere. With over 40 years of experience in the restaurant industry, we can proudly say that La Piazza has developed into one of the best Italian restaurants in town.

Using the freshest ingredients and traditional cooking techniques, our food rarely disappoints. We offer a wide range of antipasti, contorni, pizza, pasta and steak dishes as well as an endless wine list, cocktails, coffee, desserts and more.

reservations

La Piazza welcomes both people who stumble open the restaurant and walk-in, as people who reserve in advance. If you want to make a reservation for up to 12 people, you can use our reservation tool below on the right or reserve via our Google page. If you wish to reserve for a group of more than 12 persons, please email lapiazza.amsterdam@gmail.com

Our restaurant can seat up to 80 guests and has two floors. Our top floor is the perfect place for group reservations and private events of any type. Further, we also do catering and delivery should you have an event at a specific location. If you have any questions and/or inquiries, please email to lapiazza.amsterdam@gmail.com
